Select this sentence to try text selection. Search for navigation to find
another chapter. Characters remain text: café, naïve, Ελληνικά, →, ≤.

The previous paragraph is practice material. With the cursor inside it, `yiw`
copies the word, `yis` copies the sentence, and `yip` copies the paragraph.
`vip` shows the paragraph selection before you copy it with `y`.

## A reference to follow

Follow [Navigation][navigation] with `f` and its hint label. Press `Ctrl-o` to
return. To try cursor following, search for `Navigation`, press `i`, move onto
the linked text, and press Enter. A search match alone does not select the
link's destination.

## A note to inspect

The built-in manual includes a PDF note for this exercise.

Type `:annotations` and Enter, or press `ga`. Select the practice note with
`j` or `k` and press `K` to read its full comment. Press Escape to close the
comment, then `q` to close the panel. A note's author or comment can also appear
in the status line when you point at it.

## A rectangle to capture

Press `R`, use `h`, `j`, `k`, and `l` to resize the rectangle, then Enter to
show its action bar. Choose Copy text and press Enter to copy the extracted text
inside it. Press Escape to close capture. See [Figures and regions][regions] for
moving the rectangle and copying an image.

This manual contains no illustrations. Use a figure in your own PDF to try
figure detection and PNG export. Built-in help permits copying and OCR, but
cannot save a region or print.
